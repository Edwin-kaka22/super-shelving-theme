## 1. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

## 2. Change of mind

If you change your mind or you are not satisfied with your purchase, you can return it to us within **14 days of receiving or collecting your order**, as long as:

- the product is in its **original condition**, unused and undamaged **[CONFIRM: and in its original packaging; and whether assembled items can be returned]**
- you contact us within the 14 days to arrange the return
- you have your order number or proof of purchase

Items with wear or damage do not qualify for a change-of-mind return.

**How the refund works**
- You send the goods back to us at your own cost, unless you collected them from our warehouse and bring them back in person.
- We refund the full purchase price, less the **original delivery and handling cost** we paid to send the order to you.
- We refund to the **original payment method** within **[X] business days** of receiving the goods and checking them. Afterpay refunds are processed through Afterpay and may take a few extra days to show in your account.
- If you paid by bank transfer, PayID or cash at pickup, we will refund in the same way, in person or by bank transfer **[CONFIRM]**.

## 3. Exchanges

If you would like to exchange a product for a different size or style:

- contact us within the 14 days
- you pay the freight to send the original product back and for us to send the new one
- you pay any difference in price **[CONFIRM: and we refund any difference if the new product costs less]**

## 4. Faulty, damaged or incorrect products

If your order arrives damaged, has a manufacturing fault, is missing parts, or is not what you ordered:

1. Contact us as soon as possible by email at Edwin_10@live.com.au, on +61 431 533 520, or through our Contact Us page.
2. Include your order number, a description of the problem, and **photos** of the damage or fault. Photos of the outside of the boxes help if the damage happened in transit.
3. We will arrange a repair, replacement or refund, depending on what you are entitled to under the Australian Consumer Law.

We cover the cost of returning goods that are faulty, damaged or not as described. Our Warranty Policy gives you extra cover for the first 12 months.

## 5. Things we cannot accept for return

We cannot accept change-of-mind returns for:

- products that are damaged, worn or modified after delivery or collection
- products that have been assembled and used **[CONFIRM]**
- **[any custom, special-order or clearance items you want to exclude]**

This does not affect your rights if a product is faulty (section 4).

## 6. How to start a return

1. **Contact us** through the Contact Us page, or by email at Edwin_10@live.com.au, with your order number and the reason for the return.
2. We will reply with the return instructions, including the address to send goods back to **[CONFIRM: Moonee Ponds warehouse, 4 Ardmillan Road, Moonee Ponds VIC 3039]**.
3. **Pack the goods securely.** Shelving is heavy and sharp-edged, so please wrap it so it cannot be damaged or hurt anyone in transit.
4. Send it by a **tracked method** and keep your tracking details. We are not responsible for goods lost in transit on their way back to us.
