## 1. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

Nothing in this policy limits those rights. Any time limits, packaging rules or conditions in this policy do not apply to your rights if a product is faulty.

## 2. Change of mind

If you change your mind or are not satisfied with your purchase, you can return it to us within **14 days of receiving or collecting your order**, as long as:

- the product is **unused, unassembled and in its original packaging**, with all parts, fittings and manuals **[CONFIRM: unassembled only?]**
- you contact us within the 14 days and **wait for our return instructions before sending anything back**
- you have your order number or proof of purchase

Products that are worn, damaged, modified, assembled or missing parts do not qualify for a change-of-mind return.

**How the refund works**
- You pay to send the goods back to us, unless you collected them from our warehouse and bring them back in person.
- We refund the purchase price, less the **original delivery and handling cost** we paid to send the order to you. **[OPTIONAL: and less a restocking fee of [X]% - other retailers charge 20-30%. Delete this line if you don't want a fee.]**
- Once your return arrives we **inspect it**. If goods are damaged, or parts are missing, or they were poorly packed and damaged on the way back, we may deduct a reasonable amount for the loss.
- We refund to your **original payment method** within **5 business days** of approving the refund **[CONFIRM]**. Your bank or card provider may take extra time to show it. Afterpay refunds are processed through Afterpay.
- If you paid by bank transfer, PayID or cash at pickup, we will refund in the same way **[CONFIRM]**.
- If you have not received an approved refund after 7 days, check with your bank first, then contact us.

## 3. Exchanges

The quickest way to swap a product for a different size or style is to return the original under section 2 and place a new order. If you would rather we handle it as a straight exchange:

- contact us within the 14 days
- you pay the freight to send the original product back and for us to send the new one
- you pay any difference in price **[CONFIRM: and we refund any difference if the new product costs less]**

## 4. Faulty, damaged or incorrect products

If your order arrives damaged, has a manufacturing fault, is missing parts, or is not what you ordered:

1. **Contact us as soon as you can** - ideally within **[7] days** of receiving it **[CONFIRM period]** - by email at Edwin_10@live.com.au, on +61 431 533 520, or through our Contact Us page. This is a request, not a deadline: it does not limit your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
2. Please include:
   - your order number
   - a description of the problem
   - **photos** of the whole product, the damaged or faulty area, the outer packaging and the shipping label
3. **Keep the packaging** until the matter is sorted out, and do not dispose of the product.
4. We may ask you to send the product back, or arrange to inspect it, before approving a remedy.
5. We will arrange a repair, replacement or refund, depending on what you are entitled to under the Australian Consumer Law.

**Unboxing video:** for large or freight-delivered orders, we recommend filming yourself opening the packaging so that the sealed boxes, the shipping label and the contents are clear. It is not required, and not having one does not affect your rights, but it helps us resolve any damage claim faster.

We cover the cost of returning goods that are faulty, damaged or not as described. If, after inspection, a product turns out not to be faulty, we may ask you to cover reasonable inspection and return costs **[CONFIRM: to the extent the law allows]**.

Our **Warranty Policy** gives you extra cover for the first 12 months.

## 5. Cancelling an order

If you want to change or cancel an order, please contact us as soon as you can.

- **Before we dispatch it,** we can usually cancel it and refund you in full **[CONFIRM: or deduct payment processing fees of about [X]%]**.
- **After it has been dispatched or collected,** it can no longer be cancelled. You can return it under section 2.

## 6. Things we cannot accept for return

We cannot accept change-of-mind returns for:

- products that are damaged, used, assembled or modified after delivery or collection
- **sale or clearance items** **[CONFIRM if you run any]**
- **custom or special-order items** **[CONFIRM if you offer any]**
- **gift cards** **[CONFIRM if you sell any]**

This does not affect your rights if a product is faulty (section 4).

## 7. How to start a return

1. **Contact us** through the Contact Us page or by email at Edwin_10@live.com.au with your order number and the reason for the return. Please do not send anything back before we reply.
2. We will reply with the **return instructions**, including the address to send goods to **[CONFIRM: Moonee Ponds warehouse, 4 Ardmillan Road, Moonee Ponds VIC 3039]**.
3. **Pack the goods securely in their original packaging.** Shelving is heavy and sharp-edged, so wrap it so it cannot be damaged or hurt anyone in transit.
4. Send it by a **tracked method** and keep your tracking details. We cannot be responsible for goods lost on their way back to us.
5. Include a copy of your order confirmation or receipt.
