## Phase 1 - Business foundations (do these first, order matters)

- [ ] **Check the name is available**: search ASIC business names, IP Australia trade marks (classes 20 shelving/furniture and 35 retail) and Google. Do this *before* spending more on branding.
- [ ] **Register the business name "Super Shelving"** with ASIC (you already have ABN 35 812 490 397; the business name is registered separately and linked to the ABN).
- [ ] **Decide on a trade mark** for the name / logo (optional but worth considering; filed with IP Australia, fee is per class).
- [ ] **Decide on GST**: your policy pages say prices are GST inclusive. You can only charge GST if you are registered for GST (compulsory at $75,000 annual turnover, optional below). Either register for GST before launch or change the wording on the policies and product pages.
- [ ] **Business bank account** (separate from personal) for Shopify payouts. The account name should match the ABN / business name that Shopify Payments verifies.
- [ ] **Insurance**: public liability and product liability (you sell load-bearing shelving - this matters). Ask the insurer about covering delivered/assembled products.
- [ ] **Business email** on your own domain (for example hello@yourdomain.com.au) - the footer currently shows a personal live.com.au address.
- [ ] **Business phone number** you are happy to publish (footer currently uses +61 431 533 520 - confirm that is the number you want public).
- [ ] **Register the domain** (a .com.au needs the business name to match, so register the business name first). Reserve the matching social handles.
- [ ] **Supplier paperwork**: written confirmation of the 2-year warranty backing from your supplier, who handles replacement parts, lead times and stock levels, and test reports/spec sheets that support the load ratings you quote (125 kg / 625 kg etc.).
- [ ] **Accountant / bookkeeping set up** (Xero/MYOB or similar, connected to Shopify and the bank feed).

## Phase 2 - Shopify account setup

- [ ] Choose and start a **paid Shopify plan** (the trial/dev store cannot take real orders).
- [ ] **Shopify Payments**: complete identity + business verification (ABN, bank account for payouts, ID). This can take a few days.
- [ ] **Afterpay** (mentioned in the policies): check whether it can be switched on inside Shopify Payments or needs a separate Afterpay application, and apply early. Remove any Afterpay mention if you will not offer it at launch.
- [ ] **PayPal** (optional): connect if wanted.
- [ ] **Store details** (Settings > Store details): legal business name, trading name, address, store email, phone, time zone, currency AUD, units (metric).
- [ ] **Taxes**: set prices as tax-inclusive, enter your GST registration, and test one order to confirm GST is shown correctly on the receipt.
- [ ] **Shipping** (Settings > Shipping and delivery): rates / zones that match the Delivery Policy page (Victoria-wide delivery, pickup, any freight surcharge, large-item/pallet freight quotes). Every product needs a correct weight and dimensions for rates to work.
- [ ] **Local pickup** location set up if you offer pickup (the policies and the "How to collect in your vehicle" guide assume you do).
- [ ] **Policies in Shopify** (Settings > Policies): paste the Returns, Delivery (shipping), Terms of Service and Privacy policies in here too. Checkout shows these links, and they must say the same thing as the website pages. **(Claude can prepare the text.)**
- [ ] **Customer accounts**: confirm they are on (the Privacy Policy says accounts exist).
- [ ] **Notification emails** (Settings > Notifications): order confirmation, shipping confirmation, refund, etc. - check the wording, logo, business name and contact email.
- [ ] **Staff accounts / 2-step verification** on the owner login.
- [ ] **Checkout**: add the logo, brand colours and any required legal links.
- [ ] **Marketing consent**: only email people who have opted in (Spam Act). Set up the sign-up form wording if you add one.

## Phase 8 - Operations (before the first real order arrives)
